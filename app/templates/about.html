About PICME

What is PICME?

PICME is an AI-powered photo retrieval system that uses advanced face recognition technology to help event attendees find their photos quickly and easily. Simply capture a selfie, and our AI will find all photos containing your face from the event.

Technology Stack

Backend
  • Flask (Python Web Framework)
  • PostgreSQL Database
  • SQLAlchemy ORM
AI/ML
  • MTCNN (Face Detection)
  • FaceNet (Face Recognition)
  • ArcFace (Face Recognition)
  • TensorFlow & PyTorch

How It Works

  1. Event Creation: Admins create events and upload photos
  2. Face Detection: MTCNN detects all faces in uploaded photos
  3. Embedding Generation: FaceNet and ArcFace generate unique face embeddings
  4. Face Matching: When users upload a selfie, the system compares it with stored embeddings
  5. Photo Retrieval: Matching photos are returned with similarity scores

Privacy & Security

  • All photos are stored securely on the server
  • Face embeddings are encrypted
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • Users can request photo deletion through admins
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transfer
