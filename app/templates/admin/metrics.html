Metric Definitions
Precision
Percentage of retrieved photos that actually contain the person (fewer false positives = higher precision)
Recall
Percentage of all photos containing the person that were successfully retrieved (fewer false negatives = higher recall)
F1-Score
Harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a single balanced metric
False Positives
Photos incorrectly identified as containing the person
False Negatives
Photos containing the person that were missed
Processing Time
Average time to generate embeddings and match faces
